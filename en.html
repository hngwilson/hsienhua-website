Ⅰ. Company Profile

Hsien Hua Frozen Factory has been constructed since 1976. Our company is located in the Chien Chen Fishing Port, Kaohsiung, Taiwan. The Director has more than 30 years of experience in handling frozen fish processing, manufacturing, and trading. Our major markets include the USA, EU, China, Korea, Thailand, and Vietnam. Since our company has outstanding credit and experience, we have become the largest buyer for tuna, pacific saury, and squid in Kaohsiung.

Ⅱ. Product Introduction

  • Squid─── Illex squid, Peru giant squid (whole round, wing, and tube)
  • Fish for human consumption─── Yellowfin tuna, Bigeye tuna, Albacore, Swordfish, Blue shark, Sailfish, Oilfish, Mackerel, Pacific saury, Fish belly, Fish roe, Wahoo.
  • Fishing Bait─── Milkfish, Muroaji, Mackerel, Sardine, Squid and Pacific saury.
